# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

First public preview.

- Minimal panel and main window inside X-Plane 12, pop-out and VR capable, UI scale.
- Spoken station names for tuned frequencies (8.33 kHz aware, nearest transmitter wins).
- Main window tabs: Flight (who to call, your airports, ATC along your route), ATC (every online
  station with search and one-click tuning), Clearance (SimBrief plan, request, clearance and
  notes), CPDLC, Messages, Events and Settings.
- "Who covers me" hint with one-click tuning.
- ATIS letter and METAR watch for your airports, with change alerts.
- Clearance & notes card, auto-filled from clearances, SimBrief, VATSIM and METARs; transponder
  check.
- SimBrief import; Hoppie CPDLC and pre-departure clearances.
- VATSIM events; pilot and ATC hours in Settings.
- Keyboard focus hand-back and clipboard support.
- Clearance request card: destination with name and country, your stand, who to call, and a
  ready-to-say request ("…, information Echo, request clearance to Copenhagen").
- Hover any station to see where its spoken name came from.
- Transmit light: the panel mark turns red while transmitting.
- Automatic CID detection from the live callsign or SimBrief plan.
- "Get set up" checklist on the Flight tab and a welcome line in the panel on first run.
- Optional xPilot companion mode: connection status, receive lights, SELCAL, traffic count and
  shared push-to-talk (read-only towards the network).
