# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

First public preview.

- Minimal panel and main window inside X-Plane 12, pop-out and VR capable, UI scale.
- Spoken station names for tuned frequencies (8.33 kHz aware, nearest transmitter wins).
- Online ATC list with one-click tuning; ATC along your route; "who covers me" hint.
- ATIS letter and METAR watch for your airports, with change alerts.
- Clearance & notes card, auto-filled from clearances, SimBrief, VATSIM and METARs; transponder
  check.
- SimBrief import; Hoppie CPDLC and pre-departure clearances.
- VATSIM events, friends online alerts, pilot hours.
- Keyboard focus hand-back and clipboard support.
- Automatic CID detection from the live callsign or SimBrief plan.
- Optional xPilot companion mode: connection status, receive lights, SELCAL, traffic count and
  shared push-to-talk (read-only towards the network).
